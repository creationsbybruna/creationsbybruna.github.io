sections:

    number: "01"
    title: "Competitive Analysis"
    paragraphs:
      - "I looked at how comparable surf camps and adventure travel brands in Central America presented themselves to first-time visitors. The better-performing sites had a consistent pattern: what's included and what the experience feels like were front and center, and the path to booking was short."
      - "The original Damo site had the information, but it was buried in a structure that assumed visitors already knew what they were looking for."
  - group: "Discovery"
    number: "02"
    title: "Site Audit"
    paragraphs:
      - "The existing site had problems in three areas: information architecture, accessibility, and visual design. The navigation didn't map to how someone actually researches a surf trip — experience and vibe first, logistics and booking second."
      - "The color palette, heavy on teal, failed contrast checks in key areas and read as outdated against the lifestyle visual language the brand was competing in. The content itself was there, but written at too high a level of assumption about what visitors already knew."
  - group: "Strategy & Design"
    number: "03"
    title: "Information Architecture & Content Strategy"
    paragraphs:
      - "I restructured the site around the sequence a prospective guest actually follows: what is this place, what does the experience feel like, where will I stay, how do I book. The navigation reflects that order."
      - "I wrote all site copy from scratch. The original content didn't give visitors enough to picture themselves there. The new copy led with experience and community, with logistics close behind, and cut anything that slowed down the decision."
  - group: "Strategy & Design"
    number: "04"
    title: "Visual Design & Booking Flow"
    paragraphs:
      - "The visual redesign moved away from the original teal palette and updated typography, color, and layout to match what the brand was actually offering: a welcoming, lifestyle-forward surf camp with real accommodation options worth showing off."
      - "Accommodation tiers (The Hostel, La Casita Blanca, Hermosa Soul) were given enough visual and copy treatment to let visitors choose confidently without needing to reach out first."
      - "The booking flow was simplified to cut the steps between interest and commitment. Clear calls to action on every relevant page, with enough context at each step that visitors weren't left guessing what came next."
